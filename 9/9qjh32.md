## Goal
Two merges naming the same entities, one by identity and one by natural key, run one after the other instead of deadlocking.

## Purpose
Imports mix identity rows and natural-key rows; a deadlock aborts a whole batch.

## Principle
Every writer of an entity takes the entity's identity stripe before any row lock; no retry loops.

## Details
Found by the independent 1.15 review (tmp/review_1_15.md B1). The stripe hashed the key position, so identity and natural-key writers took disjoint stripes and crossed on FOR UPDATE. Fixed in 98c3a82: a natural key also locks the identity stripe of each target entity it finds.
